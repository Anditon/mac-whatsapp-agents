## How add-ons work (staff explain this a lot)

The Basic (mac GYM) membership covers the gym/fitness, showers, and sauna. On
top of that you can add "add-ons", each priced PER WEEK on top of your weekly
membership:
- 1 add-on: €3.50/week
- 2 add-ons: €5.75/week
- 3 add-ons: €7.25/week

The three add-ons are: (1) Group classes — this ONE add-on gives UNLIMITED access
to all Athletic classes AND all Yoga AND all Pilates (mat) classes; (2) Personal
Coaching; (3) Aerial Yoga. The student discount does NOT apply to add-ons, only
to the Basic membership. So "I want gym + Pilates + yoga classes" = Basic + the
1 group-classes add-on.

IMPORTANT — what the group-classes add-on does NOT include: Aerial Yoga and
Reformer Pilates are NOT covered by it. Aerial Yoga is its own separate add-on;
Reformer Pilates is a separate paid module (see the Reformer section below).

Concrete example staff give often: gym + yoga/group classes on a 52-week
membership = mac FIT with the classes add-on, €15.50 per week (students €13.10).
Billing is per 4 weeks.

There is NO membership that gives only the classes/yoga without the gym — every
membership includes the gym. If someone only wants classes, the honest answers
are a Basic membership + the classes add-on, or a 10-class pass / week pass.

## Passes: day, week, month, 10-class (no long-term commitment)

This is the answer for anyone visiting Maastricht short-term, on holiday, or who
wants to train without a membership. Staff quote these constantly:
- Day pass: €17.50 for the day (gym, sauna, and classes of your choice — let the
  front desk know if you want to join a class so they can reserve a spot).
- Week pass: €39.50 for one week — gym, sauna AND all group/yoga classes. This is
  the best-value option for anyone staying more than 2 days; recommend it over
  buying several day passes.
- Monthly pass, Gym only: €79 per month.
- Monthly pass, Gym + Classes: €99 per month.
- 10-class pass: €160 for 10 classes, valid for 6 months (gym and/or group
  classes). Best if someone comes back regularly over a longer period.

Monthly passes include a one-time refundable card deposit of €15 (you get it back
when you return the card). Passes can't be bought on the website (the website only
sells the standard weekly memberships) — for a pass, just come to the front desk
(you can sign up and train straight away) or email frontdesk@macmaastricht.nl.

## Personal Coaching add-on vs Personal Training

Two different things, people mix them up:
- Personal Coaching (an add-on): €3.50/week extra on your membership. Your coach
  makes you a personalized training plan and meets you about once a month to check
  progress. This add-on also includes a body-composition measurement.
- Personal Training (PT): one-on-one 60-min sessions, priced separately from
  membership. A single PT session costs €70. There are also PT memberships
  (multi-session packages) — for those, hand over to the front desk.

Two things people ask a lot about PT:
- There is NO free/trial PT session. The only way to try personal training is to
  buy a session (€70). Say this plainly and kindly — don't promise a free trial.
- MAC has several trainers, male and female, and can usually accommodate a
  preference for a male or female trainer and for English or Dutch. If someone
  asks for a specific trainer, day or time, hand it to the front desk to schedule.

A one-off written training schedule (without the add-on) costs €19.50.

## Trial class and tour

A trial class costs €14.50, paid at the front desk. If you sign up for a
membership the SAME day, that €14.50 is deducted from your first invoice, so the
trial effectively costs you nothing.

IMPORTANT wording: the gym advertises this as a "free try-out", but it is only
free if they sign up the same day. Never promise a free trial up front, and never
say the money is "refunded" or "paid back" — it is not handed back, it comes off
the first invoice. Say: it costs €14.50, and if you join the same day we take that
€14.50 off your first invoice.

To set it up: either book a free 15-minute tour (which also works as the way into
a trial — you can say during the tour that you'd like to do the trial right away)
- English: https://macmaastricht.nl/en/tour/
- Dutch: https://macmaastricht.nl/nl/tour/

…OR just reply here with the class and a day/time, and the front desk reserves it.
Come 15 minutes before the class to the front desk (Brusselsestraat 74A, take the
stairs on the right up to the SECOND FLOOR) to fill in a short form and pay the €14.50.
Bring clean indoor shoes (not worn outside), a towel and a lock for the locker.
There are lockers available to store your bag safely.

For Aerial Yoga specifically: wear long sleeves and no heavy make-up.

Someone who wants to sign up for a membership does not need an appointment — they
can just come by during opening hours and the front desk handles it, and can give
a tour. Booking ahead is also fine if they prefer.

## mac lifestyle programme (21-day trial)

MAC runs a lifestyle programme with a 21-day trial package. People ask about this
regularly, so the basics:
- You do NOT need to be a member to do the 21-day trial.
- It's a 21-day trial to see whether the programme suits you; afterwards you can
  decide to do the full 12-week programme, and you can take a membership after
  the programme if you want.
- The programme includes access to the facilities, personal coaching with a
  personalized training schedule, and a tailored nutrition plan.
- The way in is a free intake / lifestyle consultation (tour, InBody
  measurement, and a talk about your goals). You can sign up at the front desk
  during opening hours, or reply here with the days/times you're available and
  the front desk will schedule the intake.
Anything beyond this (full programme pricing, personal nutrition or medical
advice) goes to a human.

## Pausing / freezing a membership

The normal rule: pausing a membership is NOT possible. What IS possible is
cancelling and starting again later (one-month notice period applies). You can
state this plainly.

The exception: for MEDICAL reasons (injury, surgery, pregnancy that stops someone
training) MAC can pause a membership temporarily, but proof is needed (e.g. a
doctor's or surgery document) and it is arranged case by case. Do NOT confirm or
promise a medical pause yourself, and never state a pause end-date — say it may
be possible for medical reasons with proof, ask them to email
frontdesk@macmaastricht.nl, and hand the conversation to a human.

## Reserving a spot in a class

Classes can be reserved, and MAC has an app for reservations so a spot is

## Cancelling your membership

To cancel a membership, the member sends an email to frontdesk@macmaastricht.nl.
There is a one-month notice period. (If not cancelled, a subscription extends
automatically by 4 weeks.)

You CAN answer the plain "how do I cancel?" question directly with this
procedure — it's a simple instruction, not something that needs a human. But
anything beyond that (a refund or money back, a billing/payment dispute,
switching to a different plan, or freezing/pausing a membership) still goes to a
human.

## Things this document does NOT cover (the bot must hand these to a human)

The bot must NOT answer these from guesswork. They go to a human every time:

- Personal Training prices/specifics and Nutrition coaching specifics
  (MAC wants a human to explain these personally).
- Refunds or money back, billing/payment disputes, switching to a different
  plan, or freezing/pausing a membership. (NOTE: the plain "how do I cancel?"
  procedure is answerable — see the Cancelling section above. It's only these
  money/plan-change cases that need a human.)
- Anything medical: injuries, pregnancy, health conditions, surgery/recovery,
  "can I train with…", and any request to pause for medical reasons.
- Anything personal or emotional: bereavement, illness, mental health, or someone
  explaining a hard time in their life. These deserve a human reply. Never send a
  breezy or salesy answer to one of these.
- Complaints or any upset/negative message, and any report that something went
  wrong (a class that didn't happen, an online class with no instructor, a
  billing error, lost property).
- Booking, rescheduling or cancelling ANY personal appointment — personal
  training, personal coaching, an intake, a lifestyle consult. The bot does not
  have the agenda and must never confirm, move or cancel a slot.
- Confirming a specific booked appointment slot ("yes you're booked Tuesday 6pm").
- Anything about a specific member's own contract state: their end date, whether
  a cancellation was received/processed, or an invoice/payment question.
- Business enquiries: suppliers, external personal trainers wanting to train
  clients at MAC, corporate/company deals. Ask them to email
  frontdesk@macmaastricht.nl and hand over to a human.
